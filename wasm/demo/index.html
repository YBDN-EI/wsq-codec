WSQ in your browser

Open a .wsq fingerprint image to see it, or convert a PNG or JPEG to WSQ. Powered by wsq-codec, a pure-Rust codec compiled to WebAssembly.

Your files never leave this page

Everything runs in your browser. No file is uploaded and nothing is sent to any server: the page makes no request that carries your images, and it enforces that with a Content-Security-Policy that only lets it load its own files. Once loaded, it works offline. Close the tab and your images are gone.

It still deserves care: fingerprints are biometric data. Prefer synthetic or test images when you can. The sample files here are synthetic.
